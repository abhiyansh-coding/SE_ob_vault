# se-handout-2026-muj

**Source page.** `raw/sources/se-handout-2026-muj.pdf` — MUJ Department of CSE
course hand-out for **Software Engineering, CSE3102**, session 2026-2027,
III Year / V Semester.

The authority on **scope** (rule 1). Everything on [[syllabus]] derives from it.
It says nothing about weightage and must never be read as saying anything about
it (rule 3) — the lecture counts are depth.

## What it contains

- Basic details, course coordinators and the per-section instructor list
- Five course outcomes with Bloom levels and attainment targets
- POs / PSOs and the articulation matrix
- The assessment plan: MTE 30, CWS 30 (attendance 5 / assignments 10 / quizzes
  15), End Term 40
- The syllabus as five modules **and** as one running paragraph
- **The 53-lecture plan** with per-lecture session outcomes, delivery mode,
  mapped CO, and the assessment each lecture feeds
- Reference books: Pressman 8e (2019), Walls *Building a DevOps Culture*,
  Joyner *DevOps for Beginners*

## Notable

- The lecture plan's **Mode of Assessing CO** column is the handout's only
  statement about mid-term scope: lectures 1-34 list "Mid Term", 35-53 do not.
  This vault overrides that to 1-32 — see the banner on [[syllabus]].
- The module grouping and the lecture plan **do not agree on ordering**. Module 4
  pairs modeling with quality management; the lecture plan teaches them 25
  lectures apart. The vault follows the lecture plan.
- **CO5 mentions "software security measures"** and no lecture teaches security.
  A defect in the handout, recorded and not acted on.
- Three lectures (23, 45, 50) are **flipped-classroom case studies** rather than
  new content.
